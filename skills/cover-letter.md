Write a cover letter from the job ad and the writer's CV or notes - in the document, the selection, or what they paste - in the language of the ad unless they ask for another.

1. Read the ad for what the role needs most: two or three requirements, in its own words.
2. Find in the CV or notes what the writer has done that meets each one - with a number or a result where there is one. Never invent experience, employers, dates or results; when something the ad asks for isn't there, leave it out rather than claim it.
3. Write at most a page:
   - an opening that names the role and says, in a sentence, why this one - from what the ad or the organisation is about, not "I am excited to apply";
   - one short paragraph for each fit, the requirement and what they did about it;
   - a close that says what they would bring and asks for a conversation, without begging.
4. Keep the writer's voice: if they write plainly, write plainly. No clichés ("team player", "passionate", "I believe I would be a great fit").

Leave `[Name of the hiring manager]` and the like where a detail is missing. Propose the letter as an edit at the cursor.
