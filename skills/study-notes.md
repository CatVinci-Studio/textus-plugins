Make study notes from the text the user points to (the selection, or the document), in its language.

1. **Main ideas**: one heading for each, in the order the text builds them; under each, the idea in two or three plain sentences and the evidence or example the text gives for it.
2. **Key terms**: each term the text defines or leans on, in bold, with its meaning in one sentence - the text's own definition where it has one.
3. **Formulas, dates, names**: kept exactly; a formula in math notation.
4. **Test yourself**: five to ten questions at the end that the notes answer - a mix of "what is", "why" and "how would you" - with the answers under a "Answers" heading after them.

Nothing the text doesn't say; mark anything unclear in it with "(unclear in the source)". Propose the notes as a new section after the text, or replacing the selection when the user asks to.
