# Writing Stats

Numbers for your writing, in the sidebar:

- **This document**: words, characters and reading time.
- **Selection**: words and characters of what you selected.
- **Today**: the words you wrote today, against a daily goal (Settings › this plugin; 500 unless you change it, 0 for none), and the days in a row you met it.
- **Whole folder**: open it to count every document of the open folder.

Chinese, Japanese and Korean are counted by character, other scripts by word. Markdown's own marks - `#`, `*`, link addresses, front matter - aren't counted.

## Tools for Textus AI

Once it is installed, Textus AI can use these in a conversation:

- `count_words`: Counts words, characters and minutes to read for a document, every document in a folder, or the document in front - a Chinese, Japanese or Korean character counts as a word, Markdown's marks don't.
