# Desk Pet

A chibi companion that keeps you company while you write. Run **Show or Hide the Desk Pet** from the Plugins menu or the command palette.

- **Who:** Moyu, Textus's own cat girl - or, while Textus AI works, the LLM-chan of the model it uses (Claude-chan for Claude, GPT-chan for GPT, DeepSeek-chan, Kimi-chan, Gemini-chan…). Pick one to keep in the plugin's settings.
- **Where:** first inside Textus's window. Drag her anywhere; right-click her to switch characters, put her on your desktop (above every window), fold her to a dot or close her. On the desktop her menu also opens Textus or Textus AI. She opens where you left her and, on the right side, turns to face the middle.
- **What she does:** writes while you type, daydreams and then dozes when you stop, is pleased when you save, leans in when Textus AI is at work, sleeps while Textus is in the background. Click her and she answers; click a few times to pet her; carry her off and she's startled. She says hello once a day, welcomes you back after a long break, asks you to rest after a long stretch and to sleep late at night, and tells you when Textus AI has edits for you to review.
- **What she says:** each character speaks in her own voice, with a few lines to pick from for each moment, in all 10 of Textus's languages (`lines/`). Speech bubbles can be turned off.

She hears only *that* something happened - you typed, you saved, Textus AI is thinking - never a word of your documents or conversations, so the plugin asks for no permissions.
