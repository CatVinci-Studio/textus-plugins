# Desk Pet

A chibi companion that keeps you company while you write. Run **Show or Hide the Desk Pet** from the Plugins menu or the command palette.

- **Who:** Textus-chan, Textus's own cat girl - or, while Textus AI works, the LLM-chan of the model it uses (Claude-chan for Claude, GPT-chan for GPT, DeepSeek-chan, Kimi-chan, Gemini-chan…). Pick one to keep in the plugin's settings.
- **Where:** on your desktop, above every window, or inside Textus's window. Drag her by the bar over her head; fold her to a dot or close her from it. She opens where you left her.
- **What she does:** writes while you type, daydreams and then dozes when you stop, is pleased when you save, leans in when Textus AI is at work, sleeps while Textus is in the background, and late at night reminds you to rest. Speech bubbles can be turned off.

She hears only *that* something happened - you typed, you saved, Textus AI is thinking - never a word of your documents or conversations, so the plugin asks for no permissions.
