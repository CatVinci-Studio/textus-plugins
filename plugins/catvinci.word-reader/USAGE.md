# Word Reader

Word documents, as they look in Word, in a tab.

- Open a .docx from the sidebar, or drag one into the window.
- It shows read-only, with its layout, tables and pictures; the bar says which page you are on.
- **Ctrl+F** (**⌘F** on a Mac), or **⌕**, finds words: **Enter** goes to the next, **Shift+Enter** the one before, **Esc** closes.
- **+** and **−** zoom, **Ctrl+0** (**⌘0**) goes back to 100%.
- **Edit as Markdown** opens it as a document you can write in.
