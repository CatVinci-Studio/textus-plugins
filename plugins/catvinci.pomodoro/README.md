# Pomodoro

A focus timer that floats over the Textus window - or over the desktop - while you write: 25 minutes of focus, 5 of rest, round and round, with a notification at the end of each.

- **Plugins › Show or Hide the Pomodoro Timer** puts it up; drag it where you like, or out of the window onto the desktop.
- A click starts it or pauses it. Its right-click menu skips to the next span or starts over.
- **Settings**: how long focus and breaks last.
- The timer keeps running across a restart of Textus.

It asks for one permission: notifications, for the end of each span.
