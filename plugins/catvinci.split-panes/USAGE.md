# Split Panes

Two documents at once.

- **Ctrl+Alt+S** (**⌘⌥S** on a Mac) splits side by side, and again goes back to one document.
- **Plugins › Split Right** or **Split Down** puts the next document beside or below this one.
- Drag the line between them to share the space; click in one to work there.
- **Side by Side ⇄ One Above the Other** turns the split, **Swap the Two Sides** changes places, **Make Both Sides Equal** puts the line back in the middle.
- **Plugins › Close Split** goes back to one.
