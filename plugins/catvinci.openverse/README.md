# Openverse Pictures

Lets Textus AI find openly licensed pictures on [Openverse](https://openverse.org) for your documents.

## How to use it

- Ask Textus AI for a real photograph or drawing of something, such as "a picture of a lighthouse at dusk". It searches Openverse (Flickr, museums and more) and places the picture with its credit line under it.
- Each credit names the creator, the licence and the source, linked to the page the picture came from.
- Only CC BY, CC BY-SA, CC0 and public-domain pictures are used. Openverse passes on each source's claim, so every licence is checked again before a picture is offered.
- Searches in English find the most.
- The plugin has no menu or settings of its own: it adds one tool to Textus AI.

## Permissions

- **Network, api.openverse.org only**: to search Openverse. No account or key is needed; Openverse allows about 200 searches a day from one address.
- **Tools for Textus AI**: so Textus AI can search for pictures.

It runs on the desktop and on iOS.

## Tools for Textus AI

Once it is installed, Textus AI can use these in a conversation:

- `find_open_pictures`: Finds openly licensed pictures (CC BY, CC BY-SA, CC0, public domain) on Openverse for a description, each with its URL and the credit line its licence asks for, to place under the picture. Use it when the user wants a real photograph or drawing rather than a generated one.
