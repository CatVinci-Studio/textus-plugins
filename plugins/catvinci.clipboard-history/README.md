# Clipboard History

What you copied or cut in your documents, newest first, in the sidebar: click one to put it in again.

- Copy or cut text in a document and it shows up in the **Clipboard History** tab of the sidebar, newest first (the last 30).
- Click an entry to put it in at the caret.
- Right-click an entry to copy it again or remove it; **Clear Clipboard History** (right-click, or the Plugins menu) empties the list.
- Only what you copy in your documents is kept, never what you copy in Settings or in other apps.
