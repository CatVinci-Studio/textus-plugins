# Clipboard History

Keeps what you copy or cut in your documents in the sidebar, so you can put it in again.

Copy or cut text in a document and it shows up in the **Clipboard History** tab of the sidebar, newest first. The last 30 are kept, also after Textus restarts. Copying the same text again moves it back to the top; very long copies (over 50,000 characters) are skipped.

- Click an entry to put it in at the caret.
- Right-click an entry to **Copy** it again or **Remove** it.
- **Clear Clipboard History** (right-click an entry, or **Plugins › Clear Clipboard History**) empties the list.

Only what you copy in your documents is kept, never what you copy in Settings or in other apps. The list stays inside Textus.

## Permissions

- Clipboard: to see what you copy in a document, and to copy an entry again.
- Edit the document: to put an entry in at the caret.
