# Graph View

Your notes and the links between them, as a graph.

- **Ctrl+Alt+G** (**⌘⌥G** on a Mac), or **Plugins › Open the Graph View**, shows the whole folder in a tab.
- The **Graph** tab in the sidebar shows the notes around the document in front.
- Both [[wiki links]] and links to other documents count. Click a note to open it; drag to move around, scroll to zoom.
- How far the sidebar's graph reaches, and whether notes with no links show, are in the plugin's settings.
