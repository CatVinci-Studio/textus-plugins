# Image Viewer

Pictures and SVG files open in a tab.

- Open a picture from the sidebar, or drag one into the window.
- Zoom with the buttons or **Ctrl+scroll** (**⌘+scroll** on a Mac); **Fit** shows it whole again.
- Zoomed in, drag the picture to look around; double-click to switch between fitting it and its own size.
- **⟳** turns it a quarter at a time - the view only, the file stays as it is.
- Keys: **+** and **-** zoom, **0** is its own size, **F** fits it, **R** turns it.
- An SVG can show its source, to read or copy.
