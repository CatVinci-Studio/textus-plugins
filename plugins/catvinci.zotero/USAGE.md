# Zotero

Cite from your Zotero library while you write.

- Needs Zotero 7 running, with **Settings › Advanced › Allow other applications on this computer to communicate with Zotero** on.
- **Ctrl+Alt+C** (**⌘⌥C** on a Mac), right-click › **Insert a Citation from Zotero…**, or the Plugins menu, opens a search of your library.
- Pick an item and the citation goes in at the caret.
- How a citation is written is in the plugin's settings.
