# PDF Viewer

Opens PDFs in a tab beside your notes, to read, search and copy from.

- Open a PDF from the sidebar, or drag one into the window. A link to a page of a PDF opens it at that page.
- Scroll through the pages, or use the arrow buttons for the previous and next page.
- Zoom with the buttons, **Ctrl+scroll** (**⌘+scroll** on a Mac) or a pinch. The pages stretch around the pointer at once and turn sharp when you stop.
- A PDF opens at the window's width and keeps to it when the window is resized. Click the percentage to switch between that and the actual size.
- Select text to copy it into your notes.
- **Ctrl+F** (**⌘F** on a Mac), or the magnifier, finds words in the PDF and marks them: **Enter** goes to the next page with them, **Shift+Enter** back, **Esc** closes.
- A PDF opens again at the page and zoom you left it at. The last 200 PDFs are remembered.
- Chinese, Japanese and Korean PDFs whose fonts aren't embedded still show their text, and scanned pages show their images. A file that can't be opened says why.

It asks for no permissions: Textus hands it the PDF you open.
